The Maltese Archipelago

Three islands, one unforgettable day at sea.

Malta, Gozo and Comino are true gems of the Mediterranean — a coastline of golden limestone cliffs, hidden caves and impossibly blue water, all within reach of a single afternoon.

Adventure Cruises has been showing visitors this coastline from the water since day one, departing daily from Sliema Ferries with experienced, licensed skippers at the helm.

Blue Lagoon

The Full Day Route, Stop by Stop

One boat, four stops, six hours at sea.

The itinerary for our Crystal Lagoon & Blue Lagoon day — followed in full whenever the weather allows, at the captain's discretion.

11:30 — DEPART

Sliema Ferries

Meeting point 11:00. Arrive early for the best seats aboard.

~30 MIN

Crystal Lagoon

First swim stop of the day, Comino's quieter cove.

~2 HOURS

Blue Lagoon

Disembark and swim in the archipelago's most famous water.

~1 HOUR

Ħalfa Rock, Gozo

A dramatic, less-crowded stop on Malta's sister island.

17:30 — RETURN

Comino Caves

A final pass by the sea caves on the way back to Sliema.
